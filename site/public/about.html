PROJECT STORY

From scattered drawings to a reusable library.

MGA-resource grew out of MGA-Designer’s need for reusable medical graphics. A paper figure often tells a complete story; a new graphical abstract needs individual parts. This library makes sources, styles and exact downloads easier to compare and use.

6 November 2026: survey the open sources, pin their revisions and collect approved files on cloud runners.

7 November: preserve originals, group variants, measure appearance and review the scope. Reusable labeled anatomy stays; complete research flows and results do not.

14 November: verify retrieval, file delivery and attribution, then publish on free infrastructure.

A library for humans and agents

Browse visually and compare candidates, or call the API for matching files and direct URLs. The component test (120/120) and the original benchmark (102/120) measure distinct things. Each work retains its own license.

项目来由

把分散插画整理成可复用的医学素材。

MGA-resource 起源于 MGA-Designer 的绘图需求。一张论文图常常已经讲完一个故事，而新的图像摘要需要能重新组合的部件。我们把查找、看图、取得对应原件和保留署名连起来，减少跨站查找的负担。

如何建成

2026 年 11 月 6 日：调查开放图库、固定上游版本，在云端分批收集。

11 月 7 日：保留原件，归并变体，测量外观，按预览判定边界。带标签的可复用解剖图保留；整张研究流程和结果图排除。

11 月 14 日：分别验证检索、文件交付和署名，随后用免费资源发布。

人工浏览与 Agent 调用

人可以查看和比较候选；Agent 可以直接检索命中的文件和下载链接。组件检索 120/120 与历史基准 102/120 是不同测试。每件作品继续遵循自身许可。
